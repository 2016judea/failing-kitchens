For refrigeration, pest control and hood cleaning trades · Minneapolis health inspections · updated Oct 18, 2026

69 Minneapolis kitchens just failed on something you fix

Every week the city's health inspectors write down what is broken in a kitchen. In the 30 days to Oct 13, 2026 they flagged 68 for a cooler or freezer out of temp, 45 for pests, 20 for a dirty hood or exhaust. Each one is a kitchen with a deadline and no one to call yet.

    Source, all public: City of Minneapolis Open Data, food inspection violations, pulled Oct 18, 2026; the newest inspection on record is Oct 13, 2026. The city posts inspections a few days after they happen. The feed starts January 1, 2025 (2236 flags at 882 kitchens).

    How a flag is routed. A violation goes to refrigeration when the inspector names a cooler, freezer, walk-in or prep unit that is out of temp or in disrepair, not food left out on a counter. It goes to pest control on evidence of pests (droppings, flies, roaches, mice) or a pest-control rule. It goes to hood cleaning for a hood, baffles, exhaust duct, grease build-up or fire suppression. The routing is a fixed set of word rules, so a few flags are missed or misfiled; the inspector's words are shown so you can judge.

    "Still open" means the inspector marked it observed (not corrected on site) and the city has not inspected that kitchen since. The city does not publish when the follow-up is scheduled. "Flagged before" counts earlier inspections since 2025 that flagged the same trade at the same kitchen.
